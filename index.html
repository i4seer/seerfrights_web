The series

Fast enough for one sitting. Creepy enough to remember.

Every book follows a different kid through a place, object, or ordinary situation that turns against them. The warning is easy to dismiss. The explanation sounds reasonable. Then the story removes both exits.

Scares and jokes share the same pages. The stories are written for young readers first, but adults who still enjoy a good dark-window moment are welcome too.
