The Seer Frights engine

The scare has rules.

Every book uses the same three promises to keep the pages moving.

1

Every chapter ends on a scare.

A hand grabs. A face appears in the window. A sound comes from under the floor. You have to turn the page.

2

Half the scares are fake.

It was the cat. It was your annoying little sister. It was the wind. Until it wasn’t.

3

The last page is the proof.

No “maybe it was nothing.” Every book ends on a twist that tells you the monster was real all along.
